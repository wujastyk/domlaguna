About

Bio, bands, and musical training

Bio

I'm a bass guitarist based on the local club circuit in Edmonton, Alberta, Canada, playing jazz, rock, and blues. Whether it's walking a swing line behind a jazz combo, locking in a fuzzed-out rock groove, or laying back in a slow blues shuffle, I care most about serving the song and the pocket. I'm always up for a session, a sit-in, or a new project — reach out on the contact page.

Bands & Projects

Over the years, as my day job took me around the world, I've supported bands like the Groove Project (Cambridge, UK), BOP (Vienna, Austria), as well as Second Shot and Cut Loose! (Edmonton, Canada). I'm currently taking time off to develop my jazz soloing skills in the woodshed.
